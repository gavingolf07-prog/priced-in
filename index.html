About Priced In

Bringing better financial intelligence to every investor.

Priced In was built around a simple idea: better investment decisions start with better information.

We founded Priced In to make comprehensive financial data, advanced stock analysis, and sophisticated research tools more accessible to investors. Financial markets generate an enormous amount of information, but finding it, organizing it, and turning it into meaningful insights can be difficult and time-consuming. We wanted to build something that changes that.

Why We Started Priced In

Our interest in financial markets and investing led us to recognize an opportunity: create a centralized platform that brings together the financial information investors need with the analytical tools to make better sense of it.

Too often, investors have to navigate multiple platforms, piece together financial statements, calculate valuation metrics, and conduct their own research across disconnected sources. We started Priced In to simplify that process through technology, comprehensive financial data, and proprietary analytical tools.

Just as important, we want to offer an affordable service that can compete with far more expensive platforms, so powerful research is not limited to those who can pay premium prices.

What We’re Building

Priced In exists to help everyday investors answer one question: is a stock’s price already telling the whole story? It turns today’s price into the growth and valuation the market is expecting, lets you set your own bear, base and bull cases, and shows what return each one would give, so you can invest on your own judgment rather than on headlines.

Priced In is designed to bring multiple dimensions of financial research together in one platform. Our focus includes:

Comprehensive Financial DataAccess to company financial statements, historical financial performance, and key financial metrics.
Advanced Stock AnalysisAnalytical tools designed to help investors evaluate companies, understand financial performance, and assess valuation.
Proprietary Research ToolsTechnology-driven analytical models and scoring systems designed to uncover meaningful insights.
Centralized ResearchA streamlined experience that brings essential financial information and investment research together.

We believe financial analysis should be more accessible, efficient, and informative without sacrificing depth.

Priced In is never finished. We are continuously updating and building to make the platform better, more useful, more powerful, and more valuable with every release.

    Financial ratios

    Company diluted shares outstanding by year

    Reported

    Weighted-average diluted shares, in millions, from the reported income statements. A falling share count (buybacks) lifts EPS; a rising one dilutes it. Shares are not projected, because EPS growth is entered directly.

    How the numbers work
    • Projected price = projected EPS × the P/E you expect at the end of that year.
    • Annualized return compares the projected price with today's price over the years since your last reported fiscal year. Dividends are not included.
    • Buy at or below is the final-year projected price discounted at your target return. Use the 3, 4 or 5 year switch to set how far out that final year is.
    • Projected revenue grows from your last reported revenue at the revenue growth rate you set for each case. It does not affect the price projection, which comes from EPS × P/E.
    • Every response loaded through Firecrawl is cached in this browser, so reopening a tab costs no calls; Search refreshes it. Download PDF saves a multi-page screenshot of the whole page, in whichever light or dark mode you are using.
    • Edit any shaded cell. Type EPS or growth in a projected year and the other one follows. Each company tab is saved separately, in this browser only. Double-click a tab to rename it.

      Glossary

      P/E ratio
      Share price divided by earnings per share. How many dollars you pay for each dollar of yearly profit.
      EPS
      Earnings per share: profit divided by the number of shares. This page uses diluted EPS.
      PEG ratio
      P/E divided by the yearly EPS growth rate. Around 1 means the price roughly matches the growth.
      Bear, base and bull
      Three scenarios for growth and P/E: a weak one, a likely one and a strong one.
      Target return
      The yearly return you want. Each case is compared with it.
      Priced in
      What the current price already expects. If your case is no better than that, there may be little upside.
      Diluted shares
      Share count including options and other claims that could become shares.
